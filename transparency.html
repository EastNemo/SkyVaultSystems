Transparency

Clear boundaries on what we collect, store, and cannot access.

Our Commitment

Transparency is a core requirement for trust. This page documents what SkyVault does and does not do when handling user data, infrastructure, and legal requests.

Data We Collect

  • Account identifier (randomly generated ID)
  • Encrypted files and encrypted metadata
  • Payment confirmation (crypto transaction reference only)
  • Operational logs required for service availability

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Email addresses or phone numbers
  • Real names or identity documents
  • File contents or decrypted filenames
  • Private encryption keys or seed phrases
  • Browsing behavior across the platform

Logging Practices

Logs are limited to infrastructure health, abuse prevention, and availability monitoring. Logs are not used for profiling, tracking, or analytics.

Where possible, logs are anonymized or aggregated and retained only as long as operationally necessary.

Jurisdiction & Data Location

Users select where encrypted data is stored, subject to available regions. Jurisdiction affects storage location but does not grant access to encryption keys or plaintext data.

SkyVault cannot decrypt user data in response to legal requests, as private keys are not in our possession.

Legal Requests

If SkyVault receives a legally valid request for user data, we can provide only encrypted data and minimal account identifiers. We cannot provide decrypted files or private keys.

Users will be notified of requests when legally permitted to do so.

Warrant Canary

As of 2025, SkyVault has not received any secret government orders, bulk data requests, or gag orders requiring disclosure of user data.

This statement will be updated periodically.

Audits & Verification

Independent audits and formal verification are planned as the platform matures. Results and scope will be published publicly when available.
