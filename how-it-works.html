How SkyVault Works

A storage system designed so your data is encrypted before it ever reaches us.

Design Overview

SkyVault is built around a simple principle: encryption must happen on your device, and private keys must remain under your control. Our infrastructure is designed to store encrypted data only, without access to decryption material.

Step by Step

1. Key Generation

Cryptographic keys are generated locally on your device during account setup. Private keys never leave your environment and are not stored on our servers.

2. Local Encryption

Files are encrypted on your device before upload. Encryption is performed client-side, ensuring plaintext data is never transmitted.

3. Encrypted Storage

Our servers receive and store encrypted files only. Without your private keys, stored data remains unreadable and unusable.

4. Controlled Access

Decryption occurs locally when you access your data. SkyVault does not participate in key recovery or decryption operations.

5. Optional Destruction

Users may configure automatic deletion rules based on time, inactivity, or download count to reduce long-term exposure.

6. Geographic Choice

You choose where encrypted data is stored. Region selection affects redundancy and availability, not encryption strength.

What SkyVault Cannot Do

  • Decrypt your files without your keys
  • Recover lost private keys or seed phrases
  • View file contents or filenames
  • Associate stored data with real-world identity
